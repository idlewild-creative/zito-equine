About ZITO Equine

ZITO Equine is a family operation — David and Miranda Zito, and their son Hudson — with operations based in Colorado and Arizona alongside partner stallion stations in Texas and Oklahoma. Beyond standing stallions, David is an active competitor himself — in September 2025, he and Clayton Williams banked $145,900 winning the Riata #9.5 on Senoritas Holly Time and Boontastik, just three years into team roping.
