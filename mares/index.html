4-Year-Old Black Mare · Co-owned by ZITO Equine & Cracklin J

A 4-year-old taking on the 6-&-Under Futurity Heading at the Old West World Championships — and leading it after three rounds. Miles Baker rides her for the Relentless Remuda, where he trains alongside Trevor Brazile.
